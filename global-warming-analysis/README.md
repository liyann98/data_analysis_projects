# Effect of Global Warming on Rising Temperatures

## Overview
Statistical analysis of 25 years of global temperature data 
to investigate the relationship between greenhouse gas levels 
and mean temperature rise across four continents.

## Dataset
Berkeley Earth — mean temperatures across cities and countries 
from 1995 to 2020.

## Statistical Methods Used
- OLS Regression (R² = 0.71)
- Hypothesis Testing (t-tests, F-tests)
- ANCOVA — Analysis of Covariance
- Logistic Regression with odds ratio estimation
- Chi-square tests

## Key Findings
- Australia, Africa, and Asia are the highest contributing 
  regions to global temperature increase
- Strong positive relationship between greenhouse gas levels 
  and rising temperatures (p < 0.001)

## Tools & Technologies
- SAS (Statistical Analysis System)
- Distribution plots, Q-Q plots, confidence interval charts
